# Payment source review guide

Findings are in section A of [the primary-source knowledge base](primary-source-knowledge-base.md), with candidate observations and exclusions in sections E and F.

The [source manifest](sources/payment/manifest.json) retains exact URLs, retrieval times and SHA-256 hashes. It covers the original x402 whitepaper, current v2 core, HTTP transport, EVM exact scheme, Bazaar guide and full ERC-8004 source. The x402 license is retained; ERC-8004 is CC0. These are research copies, not installed dependencies.

Core/HTTP/exact-EVM and the full EIP were reviewed. Other network bindings, advanced payment schemes, production SDK versions, deployed contracts, audits and all optional extensions were not exhaustively reviewed. The Bazaar guide/core sidechannel difference remains unresolved. A failed schemes-overview URL in the manifest is not reviewed evidence.
